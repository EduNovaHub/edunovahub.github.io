Chapter 1: Rational Numbers

Rational numbers are numbers that can be written in the form p/q, where q ≠ 0.

Examples: 1/2, 3/4, -5/6

Properties

1. Closure Property
2. Commutative Property
3. Associative Property
4. Distributive Property

Formula

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd
